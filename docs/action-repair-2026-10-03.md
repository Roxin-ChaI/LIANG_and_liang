## Findings and changes

1. **Gu/Feng drag, both directions:** the last cell had 220 ms dwell versus 120 ms for other cells, causing a pause every loop. Both directions now use 120 ms per cell. Pointer input previously discarded individual deltas below 4 px, so slow movement could fail to enter Drag. Direction now accumulates displacement with hysteresis; repeated direction and small reverse jitter do not restart it. All eight original poses and ordering remain intact. Original artwork still has uneven leg-lift/stance changes (notably Feng 03→04→05); timing correction is **not** a claim that the underlying gait has passed visual review. No extra interpolated poses were fabricated.
2. **Feng drag fringe:** remove only bright neutral boundary pixels adjoining transparency, in two constrained passes; protect skin/hand/cuff neighborhoods. No recoloring, scaling, or smoothing. Difference masks retained per cell.
3. **Waving fringe:** same operation for both forms. White shirt/cuff content is not blanket color-keyed. Across all affected drag/waving cells, 3,332 boundary pixels were removed. Human review remains required to confirm no intended outline detail was lost.
4. **Gu Failed/Waiting eyes:** selected repair strips copied byte-for-byte from `gu-eye-repair-2026-10-01`. They use the already-approved Gu work-state baseline correction, so this build starts from `assets/rework/v0.1.0/gu/spritesheet.webp`, not the old five-pixel-high work rows.
5. **Gu/Feng Look shoulders:** directional upper body slices had been joined to a fixed lower torso with a horizontal cut, producing shoulder protrusions/seams. Reuse each form's complete Up shoulder/collar/body and transfer direction-specific head pixels in place using a face/neck silhouette mask, rather than a rectangular shoulder cut. All 16 directions retain their original upper-head pixels; lower body/shoulders are shared. Contact sheets reviewed; full aesthetic approval is left to the user.
6. **Gu→Feng:** user-selected “historical trial 2”, ten frames from `transition-full/gu-to-feng-retry/frames`, copied exactly into the existing 224×240 overlay canvas at (16,18). No image scaling or player changes. Existing historical pose/head movement imperfections are retained, not represented as newly approved. Feng→Gu remains unchanged.
