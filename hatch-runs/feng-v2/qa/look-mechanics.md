# 梁文峰 look mechanics

- Stable anchor: both feet, legs, torso, arms, hands, suit silhouette, body center, shoulder width, and baseline remain fixed.
- Gaze lead: pupils, eyelids, and eyebrows establish the target direction first while preserving the thin-frame glasses and cold expression.
- Head/neck follow-through: the head and neck may make only a restrained rigid turn or pitch; no skull scaling, raster warp, or whole-body tilt.
- `000 Up`: pupils move upward, upper eyelids open slightly, chin rises minimally; face remains broadly frontal.
- `090 Right`: pupils and nose/face axis shift toward screen-right; the screen-left side of the face becomes marginally more visible.
- `180 Down`: pupils move downward, eyelids narrow, chin lowers minimally; strong posture remains intact.
- `270 Left`: pupils and nose/face axis shift toward screen-left; the screen-right side of the face becomes marginally more visible.
- Motion budget: cardinal changes are confined to the head/eye area with at most a few pixels of neck/shoulder accommodation. Head dimensions, shoulder width, body scale, and registration are identical in every cell.
